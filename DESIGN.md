# UI / motion design spec — "Force-directed placement" lesson video

Canvas **1920×1080, 30 fps**, drawn as one SVG. Everything is generated by `frame(t)` in `index.html` / `index_full.html`
(`t` = seconds of the original narration). Audience: young learners → bright, rounded, friendly, big type.

| Pastel card fills | `#FFE9A8 #C9F2DC #D3E1FF #FFD3E3` | bullet cards 1–4 (paired with dark badges `#FFB300 #2BBF7E #4C7DFF #FF5C93`) |

**Type:** Poppins 700 (titles 72 px, card titles 34–38, badges), Poppins 500 (body 31–40 px), DejaVu Sans Mono Bold 35–38 px (code).
**Shape:** cards radius 36, pills radius = height/2, soft shadow `dy 8, blur 9, #1F2A5A @16%`, white 5 px outline on coloured pills.

## 2. Persistent layers (every scene)
1. **Background:** sky gradient, 4 drifting clouds, 14 slow rising pastel bubbles, two green hills (y≈1000+).
2. **Progress bar** (y=30–48, x=120–1800): rainbow fill, chapter dots, spinning gold star at the playhead.
3. **Header** (x=110, baseline y=150): section title 72 px bold, pops in (back-ease), accent underline grows.
4. **Content area:** x 90–1830, y 170–870. Bottom band y 890–1040 is reserved for mascot + speech bubble.
5. **Mascot "Pip"** (x≈1795, on hill): yellow round cell, antenna, blinking eyes, moods: smile / "o" / worry / wave / cheer (jumps).
   Speech bubble (34 px) shows at section start +0.8 s for ~4.6 s.
6. **Transition:** each section slides in from +70 px and fades in over 0.45 s; a ring of 12 coloured stars bursts near the mascot.

## 3. Motion rules
- **Pop-in:** scale 0→1 with back-ease overshoot (`c1=1.70158`) over 0.45 s + 0.18 s fade. Used by cards, chips, bullets.
- Ease-out cubic for slides/underline; ease-in-out for node moves (`ez`); ping-pong loops for demos.
- Idle life: nodes bob ±3 px, wires carry travelling dots, dashed "wire box" marches, mascot bounces, bubbles float.
- Reveal times come from the narration's sentence starts (detected from silences) — see `SCH` and `SEC` at the top of the file.

## 5. Graph board component (`drawBoard`)
Board card with 0.5-unit grid; nodes A–F as 36 px circles with letters; 8 wires (A–B, A–F, F–C, F–E, A–E, E–D, C–D, B–D);
dashed orange **wire box** = bounding box of all cells (shrinks as wires get shorter); optional ghost circles + arrows showing the previous
positions. Four stage layouts `P0 start / P1 mid / P2 after-force / P3 golden` (+ `PC` collapsed) are in unit coordinates 0–2 — approximations
redrawn from the original slides.

## 6. Customising
- Change colours: tokens at the top of `index.html` (`INK`, `PAST`, `DARK`, `COL`, `SEC[].acc`, `DEFS` gradients).
- Change copy: strings inside each `scenes[n]` function. Change timing: `SEC` (section start/end) and `SCH` (reveals).
- Add a scene: add an entry to `SEC` with `sc:<index>` and a `scenes[index]=(t,sec)=>…` function returning SVG markup.
