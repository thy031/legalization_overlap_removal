Pitfalls to Watch

Trap 1 · Snap ≠ Legal: Snapping puts A, B, C on integer grid sites at (4, 2), but all three still overlap until Phase 2 left-packs them.
Trap 2 · Ignoring Cell Width: Each width-2 cell occupies [x, x + 2)—two consecutive sites—not a zero-width point.
Trap 3 · Cross-Row Jumps: Overlap removal is an in-row x interval fix; cells stay on their assigned row y.
Trap 4 · Overflow & Macros: Never overwrite fixed macros like D@(8,4), and flag failure/overflow whenever packing exceeds chip width W.
